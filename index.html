Frequently asked questions

Good questions. Clear answers.

Understand the process before committing. Your questions are welcome, even if you are not sure what technology you need.

Tell us what you have in mind
Do I need to know which software or website to choose?+

No. Tell us what you do, who will use it and what problem you want to solve. We can suggest a practical first version and explain the options in plain language.

How is the project price decided?+

We prepare a project-specific quote based on pages, features, integrations, design and support needs. The proposal should make the scope, payment stages and any recurring costs clear before work begins.

Will my website work on mobile phones?+

Responsive layouts are part of our website approach. Exact device support, browser requirements and acceptance checks are agreed in the project scope.

What about domains, hosting and future updates?+

We discuss domain ownership, hosting arrangements, ongoing charges, backups and maintenance separately, so you know what is included and what needs renewal.

Which VBT education products are available?+

VBT Smart School is our ready Windows offline school management product. VBT Smart Campus, VBT Paper Generator and VBT Quiz Maker are under development; please contact us about demonstrations or availability.
